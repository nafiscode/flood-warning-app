- 2026-10-09 — **Jaga makes five sounds** (owner: notifications should have sounds, urgent for SOS and positive for rescued or safe, and a distinctive one of its own for a dam release), all generated in the app with the Web Audio API — no audio files, so nothing to download, cache or licence, and they work offline. An urgent two-tone alarm that repeats until answered, for an authority being offered a case; a short rising pair for the requester when a team accepts; three soft notes resolving downward for "rescued" and "I'm safe now"; one low double-tone for a new alert at Warning or Evacuate; and, for a dam release notice (spec section 15), a rising figure **repeated**, used for nothing else — the only public alert sound that repeats, because the water is already on its way. **Watch and Normal are silent.** Every sound is paired with a vibration pattern and with its own icon and words on screen, so sound is never the only channel (accessibility rule). Two honest limits, written into docs/brand.md: a browser plays nothing until the person has tapped once, so the authority console asks once to arm the alarm and stays armed; and a web push arriving with the app closed plays the phone's own notification sound, which no web app can replace. — A dispatch offer with a 60-second window is worthless if the phone stays quiet, and the sound of good news should not be the sound of an alarm.
- 2026-10-09 — **A dam release notice reaches the tributaries too, and sounds like itself** (owner, adding to the Bang Lang decision of today). The zone is no longer only the tambons along the Pattani River below the dam: it includes **the lower reaches of the streams that join it**, where a rising main stem backs water up into the tributary instead of letting it drain, so people on a side stream are warned as well. S7 defines that zone and its arrival ranges. The notice carries its own sound (spec 4.8, docs/brand.md), distinct from the alert double-tone and used for nothing else, and it is the one public alert sound that repeats. Sent by a verified RID or EGAT operator opening the gates or raising the spillway flow, and by admins from the same template until an operator has joined. — The owner's point: a release floods the side streams as well as the main channel, and the sound has to tell someone who is asleep that this one is water on its way, not a forecast.
