- 2026-10-21 — **The public map grades a river gauge by that gauge's own levels, and a gauge at or over its bank gets its own word** (owner, after spotting it: "one says in the Map as almost reach the river bank but on the Signals page doesn't seem to show the same"). Two gradings had drifted apart. `station_status()`, which the public map reads, graded on the `stations` columns: red at `warning_m` (the bank) and amber at `coalesce(watch_m, warning_m - 0.5)` — and `watch_m` is set on only 2 of the 48 gauges, so for every other one "close to its bank" was the flat guess of half a metre below it. The admins' signals meanwhile grade on `public.gauge_levels`, which holds a Watch, Warning and Evacuate level per gauge (from `thresholds gauges`, and from an admin through `admin_set_gauge_levels`). Measured on jaga-dev across the 46 gauges that have both: the map turned amber later than the real Watch on 34 of them (0.69 m late on average, 4.74 m at worst), red later than the real Warning on 39 and never earlier, and an admin's own levels never reached the public map at all. So `gauge_levels` is now what the public map reads (migration `..a10_station_status_gauge_levels`), with the `stations` columns kept as the fallback for a gauge with no row there; the function is `security definer` because `gauge_levels` is admin-read-only, and no level value leaves it — only the status word, and the bank level that was public before. Because Evacuate equals the bank on all 46 gauges and Warning sits below it on all 46, there are three live bands where the map drew two, so the map gained a fourth status, `above_evacuate`, rather than folding it into `above_warning`: a level is never shown lower than it is (safety rule 3). The three bands now read "above its normal level", "close to its bank" and "at or over its bank", a gauge mark gained a sixth look (`high`, a glass just over half full, no ripple), and the public wording still avoids the words Watch, Warning and Evacuate, because a gauge reading is a measurement and never an alert (safety rule 2). The monitoring card calls a river an event from the Warning band up: above its normal level is counted and reported, not announced.
